Desktop 12-column grid; tablet 6; mobile 1–2 columns. Sticky mobile bottom navigation for Home, Orders, Products, More; store switcher at top. Long forms use sections and sticky save bar; sheets for filters and quick actions; full-page detail for complex tasks. Writing, states and quality

Use plain merchant language: “Orders”, “To fulfil”, “Add product”, “Connect payments”, “View store”, “Request support”. No generic marketing banners, repeated greetings, fake AI copy or decorative charts. One primary action per page; use action verbs and explain consequences. Empty state: “No orders yet. Orders will appear here when customers check out.” Payment disabled: “Connect Stripe to accept payments.” Failure: “Refund could not be completed. No refund has been confirmed. Try again or contact support.” Separate pending from failed; display last updated and retry. Confirm destructive actions with exact object and impact. Every widget must answer a question or lead to action; optional rearrangement later, never hide critical alerts. Use skeletons, empty, partial, error, permission-denied and offline states. WCAG 2.2 AA, visible focus, keyboard navigation, screen reader announcements, non-colour status indicators, contrast and reduced motion.
